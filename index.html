The OC Campus Collective is a network of churches and ministries serving college and high school students across Orange County, run by Cru and Trellis.

What we believe

Every college and high school student in Orange County should have the opportunity to encounter Jesus through a local Christian community.

From UC Irvine and Cal State Fullerton to Chapman, Saddleback and the high schools in between, there are more campuses in Orange County than any one ministry can reach. By investing in one another, sharing resources, and collaborating across organizations, we can accomplish far more together than any of us could alone.

What we ask of partners

The Collective works because people show up for each other. We ask participating organizations to:

  • Regularly attend OC Campus Collective gatherings
  • Build relationships with other churches and ministries serving students
  • Share ideas, encouragement and best practices
  • Celebrate wins and collaborate toward reaching every campus in Orange County

Up to $1,500 for each new campus launch.

The Collective is investing in organizations launching ministry on college and high school campuses where no Christian presence currently exists.

Rather than one-time funding, the grant is an invitation to become part of a network that shares ideas, resources, encouragement and strategy for years to come.

Funds may be used for

  • Student outreach events
  • Promotional materials and printing
  • Interest meetings and hospitality
  • Leadership development resources
  • Campus registration fees and launch supplies
  • Other approved launch-related expenses

How to apply

Email Ainsley. Tell her which campus, which organization, and what you are planning. She will take it from there.
